# Project Planning Acceptance

## Objective
Approve a coherent whole-project direction without converting tentative discussion into committed scope.

## Pass conditions
The user approves the outcome, MVP boundary, and milestone order; all milestones are outcome-based and the first step is unambiguous.

## Required evidence
Record approved decisions, controlled plan records, referenced criteria, dependency checks, and the first deterministic step.

## Waiver conditions
Use the core waiver format; name the unavailable primary evidence, alternative evidence, and residual risk. A waiver cannot conceal an unresolved scope or decision-owner problem.

## Failure conditions
Tentative ideas are recorded as approved, MVP and non-goals are absent, or no first deterministic step exists.
